How to Play

The deck: 40 cards — Aces count 1, number cards count face value. No Jacks, Queens or Kings.

The goal: capture cards, then win the scoring categories at the end. 11 points are in play for 2 players and for the 4-player pairs game; 7 points for 3 singles. The four-hand game is always played in pairs — opposite players are partners.

The turn: a turn is a series of moves and only ends when you press End Turn. Exactly one move per turn may use a card from your hand — digs, folds and scaffold foundings use no hand card and may come before or after it. You cannot end the turn before your hand card has been played.

  • Capture — take one set of discard-area cards whose values sum exactly to your card — or take a build with a card of exactly its value. A build is never part of a sum: a 7-build falls only to a 7, never to a 10 padded with table cards. The game takes exactly what you highlighted — nothing more.
  • Build — play a hand card onto discard-area cards and declare the total. The build registers to its owner (who must hold a card of that value) and stands in their area. No two live builds share a value, and each player holds one build (in pairs, briefly two — the second only via Shiya, and one must be captured before that turn ends).
  • Top / Augment — grow your own side's build without changing its value: top it with an equal card, or fold table cards in. A hand card never enters an opponent's build. Once augmented, a build's value locks forever.
  • Preg — raise an opponent's unaugmented build with a hand card alone (never your own build, never a partner's, never with table cards). If the new value is free the build becomes yours; if your own side already holds that value live, the enemy build folds into it and it locks; if your partner virtually owns the value, the build restarts as his.
  • Scaffold — found a stack from table cards alone, of a value you hold. It is not a build: it stands in the discard area, registered to nobody, and must be captured (with your held value) or topped into a live build before the turn ends. A base card of the value folds beneath automatically; founding with an opponent's pile top requires that base.
  • Discard — drop a card into the discard area. While any build is live, cards of its value may NOT be discarded — capture, top, or keep them. In the two-hand game's first round, a player with a live build cannot discard at all.
  • Shiya (pairs only) — when your partner captures and you hold the matching value, you may call "leave it": the captured set becomes a live build registered to you, standing in your build area.

End of the game: when the stock and all hands are empty, the last player who captured sweeps any leftover table cards. Then score:

  • Most cards — 2 pts  ·  Most spades — 2 pts (2-player / partnership games only)
  • 2 of Spades (“spy two”) — 1 pt  ·  10 of Diamonds (“mummy”) — 2 pts  ·  each Ace — 1 pt

Ties in a “most” category score nothing for anyone; a tied game is a shared win.
