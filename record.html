🎤 Record My Table

How to get great card sounds: find a quiet room. Lay the phone flat on the table, about a hand's width from where the cards will land, microphone side toward them. Then for each sound below: tap Record, do the thing once, clearly, and tap Stop (or let it stop itself). Play it back — if you like it, it's already saved as yours. Record as many takes as you want; the newest take wins. Your recordings are used by the game on this phone immediately — refresh the game after recording. The Download link lets you send a take to me later so it ships to every player.
