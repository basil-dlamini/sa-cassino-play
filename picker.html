🎚️ Sound Picker

Every move of the game, and every recording in the library. Pick the sound you want for each move (the game's current one is preselected and marked), tap Play to hear it at the volume that move actually uses, and when you're done press Copy my choices and paste the result to the assistant — your exact map gets wired into the game for everyone. Your own studio recordings appear at the top of every list as ★ My … recording — and where you have one for that very move, it is preselected. If you pick a ★ recording for any move, also grab its ⬇ Download from the studio page so it can ship to every player.
